How To Play Piano Master

Learn the rules once, then use the ? Inspector whenever you need a quick reminder.

The rule of the game

Look at the hand, find the starting finger or note, follow UP or DOWN, and choose where you land.

RH = Right Hand
LH = Left Hand
Finger 1 = Thumb
Finger 5 = Pinky
↑ UP = notes move higher
↓ DOWN = notes move lower

✋ Finger Numbers

Both hands use 1–5: thumb is 1, index is 2, middle is 3, ring is 4, and pinky is 5.

↕️ UP / DOWN

UP and DOWN follow the notes on the piano. On RH, UP moves toward the pinky; on LH, UP moves toward the thumb. DOWN reverses that.

🎹 Piano Challenges

The mini piano always shows C–D–E–F–G–A–B–C. Read the hand, reference finger, starting note, and direction, then tap the note where you land.

✋ Finger Challenges

The gold finger is your starting point. Follow the UP or DOWN clue by the number of fingers shown, then tap the finger where you land.

🎯 Practice Mode

Explore without a timer. Change hand, starting finger, direction, and interval to see the relationship before you are tested on it.

⚡ Challenge Mode

Solve mixed piano and finger questions before time runs out. The bottom Interactive Piano can also answer note questions if you prefer the larger keyboard.

⌨️ Computer Keyboard

For piano answers, press C–B (C D E F G A B) on your typing keyboard. For finger answers, press 1–5. You can also use ←/→ to move between choices and Enter to select.

🔥 Streaks & Bonuses

Correct answers in a row build your streak. Bonus points and time rewards are for fun only—they never skip musical curriculum progress.

📈 Musical Progress

Notes, scales, titles, and curriculum content follow actual learning progress. Game reward points stay separate so practice cannot unlock material early.

📊 Skill Feedback

The Scale Progress Book tracks accuracy, speed, finger recognition, note recognition, and consistency. Tap a skill card to understand what to improve.

❓ Inspector

Tap a ? button or a Quick Key item for a short explanation without leaving the activity.
