How To Use Piano Master

Learn how to use the piano, your hands, and the clues in each challenge.

Total Score

Your total score counts the successful challenge answers you earn while practicing.

Game Level

Your game level grows as you earn points. This shows your game progress and is separate from your musical curriculum level.

Streak

Your streak counts consecutive correct answers. Keep going to build your best streak!

Practice Mode

Practice Mode lets you explore notes, hands, fingers, and movement without a timer. Use the arrows and piano highlights to see how the musical relationship works.

Challenge Mode

Challenge Mode gives you musical questions to solve. Some questions ask you to find a note on the piano, while others ask you to identify a finger.

✋ Look At The Hand

Always check whether the question shows your Right Hand or Left Hand. Finger numbers work in opposite directions between the two hands.

🎹 Find The Starting Point

Find the starting finger or starting note shown in the question. Your starting position can change as you progress through the curriculum.

⬆️ Follow The Direction

Use the arrow or direction clue to know which way to move from your starting position.

🎯 Find Your Destination

Put all the clues together: hand, finger, starting note, and direction. Then choose the note or finger where you should land.

📈 Unlock More As You Learn

As you progress, more notes, positions, and musical material can become available. Your teacher controls curriculum progression so you can learn at the right pace.

Scale Progress Book

The Scale Progress Book shows the musical material you have unlocked through your curriculum progression.

Interactive Piano

When a challenge asks for a note, use the clues to figure out the answer and tap that piano key. The piano will play the note you select.

Finger Questions

When a challenge asks for a finger, look at the hand shown and use the starting position and direction to determine the correct finger.

Application Activity

The activity panel shows what is happening during your session, including answers, progress, and system events.

Achievements

Achievements recognize milestones as you continue practicing and successfully completing challenges.

Adjust curriculum restrictions and view basic performance diagnostics.
